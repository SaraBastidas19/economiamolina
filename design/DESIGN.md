## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a warm, approachable, and commercial retail presence rooted in over a quarter-century of footwear expertise in downtown Pasto, Nariño. The interface balances established commercial trust with modern digital purchasing habits. The aesthetic fuses contemporary Colombian retail efficiency with tactile warmth—reassuring, unpretentious, and community-centric.

The visual direction combines **Modern Retail** with **Subtle Tactile Warmth**:
- Clean surface hierarchy using warm cream canvas tones rather than clinical cold whites.
- Deep, grounded navy slate foundational tones to convey stability and institutional reliability.
- Warm amber/gold accents highlighting seasonal craft, artisan leatherwork, and family heritage.
- Purposeful high-conversion triggers via vibrant WhatsApp emerald accents for direct, familiar customer communication.
- Soft, inviting geometry with generous radius values and pill-shaped touch targets that feel friendly across all age groups and devices.

## Colors

The color palette centers on dependability, local affinity, and commercial legibility:

- **Primary (`#1a365d`) & Navy Deep (`#0f2444`)**: Anchors structural navigation, top app bars, primary action links, and core headers. Evokes the longevity, honor, and reliability of a family establishment.
- **Secondary (`#d97706`) & Heritage Gold (`#d4a038`)**: Accentuates seasonal sales, promotion tags, leather craft callouts, star ratings, and loyalty markers.
- **Tertiary (`#128c7e`) & WhatsApp Vibrant (`#25d366`)**: Reserved strictly for conversational conversion paths—"Pedir por WhatsApp", stock inquiries, click-to-chat catalog entries, and immediate checkout support.
- **Canvas Cream (`#fbfbfa`) & Surface Warm (`#f4f1ec`)**: Forms the ambient stage, giving product photography—particularly natural leathers and textiles—a warm, true-to-life baseline.
- **Text Primary (`#1a1a1a`) & Alert Terracotta (`#c43726`)**: Provides high-contrast readability for Colombian Peso prices (`$ COP`) and urgent stock/clearance tags.

## Typography

The type scale combines **Outfit** for titles, banners, and prominent numeric price tags with **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body descriptions, transactional parameters, and functional interface components.

- **Numerics & Prices**: Colombian peso values (`$ 189.900 COP`) are styled using `price-display` in Outfit Bold. Sizing ensures immediate visual parsing while scanning product feeds.
- **Editorial Voice**: Category titles and promotional banners leverage Outfit Semi-Bold to project warmth without sacrificing retail precision.
- **Accessibility & Scanning**: Product specs (shoe sizes, upper materials, sole characteristics) use `body-md` and `label-md` with comfortable optical tracking for effortless legibility across budget and flagship mobile devices alike.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured responsive grid provides balance for content-heavy footwear catalogs:

- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Product listings display primarily as a 2-column card matrix with minimum touch targets of 48px to accommodate one-handed browsing on busy transit routes.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Supports split-screen filtering and 3-column catalog grids.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout capped at 1280px maximum content container, with `3.5rem` outer margins and `2rem` gutters. Allows persistent sidebar filtration (by shoe size, brand, gender, and price range) alongside a 3 or 4-column product grid.

Component padding applies `space-sm` inside compact interactive chips and `space-lg` to `space-xl` for card modules and conversion banners.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy employs ambient, warm-tinted shadows rather than sharp black drop-shadows, creating soft, tangible layers reminiscent of a curated storefront:

- **Canvas (Level 0)**: `#fbfbfa` base tone with zero elevation.
- **Card Rest (Level 1)**: Crisp white surface (`#ffffff`) surrounded by a subtle warm perimeter: `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 36, 68, 0.06), 0 1px 3px rgba(15, 36, 68, 0.04)`. Outlined with an ultra-light border (`1px solid #f0ede6`).
- **Interactive Hover & Dropdowns (Level 2)**: Slightly lifted state for hovered product cards and active filter popovers: `box-shadow: 0 10px 22px -6px rgba(15, 36, 68, 0.10), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 36, 68, 0.05)`.
- **Sticky Actions & Conversions (Level 3)**: Bottom sticky purchase bars, floating WhatsApp trigger buttons, and cart drawers: `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -8px rgba(15, 36, 68, 0.16), 0 6px 14px -4px rgba(15, 36, 68, 0.08)`.
- **Modals & Lightboxes (Level 4)**: Size guide modals and zoomed shoe inspections: `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 36, 68, 0.22)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an inviting rounded aesthetic (`roundedness: 2`):

- **Product Cards & Banners**: 12px to 16px border radius (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`) to soften boundaries and highlight clean silhouette footwear photography.
- **Buttons & Interactive Tags**: 10px on standard buttons; full pill-shape (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for search inputs, category badges, size selector chips, and the prominent floating WhatsApp contact button.
- **Inputs & Form Controls**: 10px corner rounding for text inputs, dropdown selects, and address fields, presenting approachable and low-friction checkout steps.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Deep Navy (`#1a365d`) background with white text, font weight 600, 10px radius, 48px standard touch height. Provides grounding and decisive intent for cart actions.
- **WhatsApp Commercial Conversion**: Emerald Green (`#25d366` or `#128c7e`) with white bold text, integrated WhatsApp icon, and subtle pulse feedback. Sized prominently across product detail views for immediate Colombian commercial communication.
- **Secondary / Subtle**: Warm cream fill (`#f4f1ec`) with Deep Navy text, or ghost outline (`1.5px solid #1a365d`).

### Product Cards
- Contained on pure white (`#ffffff`) surfaces over the warm cream canvas.
- Features: Aspect ratio 1:1 or 4:5 for footwear imagery, upper-left badge for "Envío Nacional" or "Pasto Inmediato", upper-right favorite heart toggle.
- Price presentation clearly highlights current sale price in bold Navy, with original strikethrough price in neutral slate (`#64748b`).

### Size & Attribute Selector Chips
- Pill or rounded 8px containers (minimum 44x44px for shoe sizes like 37, 38, 39, 40).
- Unselected state: Crisp white background with `#e2ded7` border and dark text.
- Selected state: Solid Deep Navy (`#1a365d`) fill with white text and active scale lift.
- Out of stock: Light gray cross-through with disabled opacity (`0.4`).

### Price Display & Badges
- Clear presentation of Colombian currency: `$ 189.900 COP`.
- Discount and Heritage Badges: Gold Amber (`#d97706`) or Terracotta (`#c43726`) pill badges with uppercase `label-xs` typography.

### Input Fields
- Generous 48px height with clean 1px border (`#e2ded7`), switching to 2px Navy (`#1a365d`) focus ring with zero optical displacement.
- Floating or persistent labels in `label-sm` to prevent context loss during mobile autofill.

### Trust Banner & Shipping Indicator
- Reassuring visual ribbon highlighting: "25+ Años en el Centro de Pasto", "Pago Contra Entrega", and "Garantía de Calidad", utilizing soft amber and slate iconography.
